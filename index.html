How to play

  1. Each player has 17 tiles in their colour plus one starting tile: Light's is the concentric squares, Dark's is the cross. Every side of a tile is either solid colour or stripes.
  2. Light starts. Each player's first move is their starting tile, placed on any of the 16 inner squares. Starting tiles can't go on the edge of the board, and there must be at least one empty square between them: they can't touch, not even at the corners.
  3. After that, place one tile per turn on an empty square next to a tile already on the board. You can turn tiles to any rotation.
  4. Every side that touches another tile must match it: stripes against stripes running the same way, or solid against the same solid colour.
  5. If you can't place a tile on your turn, you lose. The last player to place a tile wins.

House rule: tiles on the edge of the board don't need to match the border. Only sides that touch another tile count.

Reading the joins

A solid side facing an empty square claims it: only tiles of that colour can go there. Use your colour to shut squares to your opponent and keep some for yourself.
